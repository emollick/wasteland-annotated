text: <p>Card XII: a young man hanging by one foot from a living tree, serene, haloed. Eliot linked him to Frazer’s Hanged God, the sacrificed king whose death brings the rain, and to the hooded figure on the road to Emmaus. Madame Sosostris does not find him. No sacrifice, no resurrection, in this deal.</p>
