## Disagreements decided for the scholar

1. line 62: the reader's "Neither is a place of punishment" not taken, with the writer's "short of its torments" that the reader had kept: the neutrals are punished, stung by hornets and wasps and running after a banner (Inferno III 52–54 and 64–69), as the checker showed; only Limbo is without torments.
2. line 62: the neutrals glossed by their place, as the checker glosses them, "in the vestibule inside the gate", and Limbo as "the first circle", rather than by the reader's "who lived without praise or blame", which the card at 63 already gives in Dante's words; with that gloss gone the reader's citation III 34–42 reverts to the checker's and the draft's III 40–42 (the sources line keeps 34–42 and 55–57).
3. line 193: "the king my father's death" is the poem's line, altered from Ferdinand's "wreck", so "in words from Ferdinand's", not the reader's "in Ferdinand's words".
