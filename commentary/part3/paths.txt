line: 300
text: Eliot spent four weeks at Margate, from 15 October to 12 November 1921, on sick leave from the bank, and drafted "some fifty lines" of Part III in a shelter on the front, probably the Nayland Rock shelter, listed in 2009, which still stands. The lines are thought to be the river song and the Thames-daughters' speeches.
sources: Letters, vol. 1, Eliot to Sydney Schiff, 4 November 1921
