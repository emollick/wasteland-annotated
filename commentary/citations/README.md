# Citations for the commentary

Written by the citations thread ("also definitely do visual checks ... also you should cite the scholarly sources you draw on", Ethan, 20 September 2026, 20:06 UTC), which is adding a `sources:` field to the commentary records.

- `BRIEF.md`: the rules the citing agents work to (cite only what was opened or can be verified; no guessed page numbers; locate by a work's own structure; short forms from the works list).
- `works.draft.txt`: the list of works cited with short forms, full forms and the open copy consulted; being verified. The settled list will be `works.txt`.
- `ol.py`: a helper that searches inside the scanned copies of the commentaries on archive.org (through Open Library) and prints snippets; how a reading is confirmed to be in Ricks and McCue, Southam or Rainey without a page number. `python3 ol.py '"phrase"' --limit 40`.
- Reports on what could not be cited will be added here, one per part, when the citations are written into the record files.

The field format is being agreed with the build thread; until then nothing in the record files has changed.
