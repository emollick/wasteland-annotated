## sappho-104

Uncited: that Stevenson's line "has been cited" as a link to Eliot's lines 220–221 (Byron's is, by Wharton, as an imitation of Sappho). Ricks and McCue, commentary on line 221, would settle it.
Error: "Two lines that survive because Demetrius ... quoted them." ## Errors found
1. sappho-104: the two lines are not preserved by Demetrius alone; Demetrius § 141 quotes the anaphora only, and the first line's second half comes from the Etymologicum Magnum (evidence: Roberts's Demetrius, 1902, § 141; Wharton, Sappho, 1887, fr. 95).
2. Works list, "Pervigilium Veneris": the passage on the record runs from line 85, not "lines 89–93".
3. Works list, "Gomme, Traditional Games": the "London Bridge" entry is pp. 333–50, not 333–49.
No other error in the notes was found; discrepancies with other accounts (Brooker's "senior", Southam's 1918 date for "Dans le Restaurant", Froude's "Cumnor Hall") are noted above and do not make the notes wrong.
