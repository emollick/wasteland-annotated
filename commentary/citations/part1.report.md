## Errors and corrections, gathered
1. woolnoth: "from 9.15 to 5" — Moody (1979) and Bantock quote Eliot's hours as 9.15 to 5.30 with one Saturday in four; the letter should be checked in Letters vol. 1.
2. woolnoth: the station works beneath the church are 1897–8 in the Historic England entry, 1897–1900 in the note.
3. sosostris: "prosecuted ... in the 1920s" is not what the cited Hansard debate (1951) says.
4. dedication: the January 1923 dating is attributed to Ricks and McCue but was confirmed only from Southam and Seymour-Jones.
5. Works list: Frazer's Adonis, Attis, Osiris is linked to the 1906 edition, whose bk. I numbering differs from the 1914 edition's ("The Gardens of Adonis" ch. IX against ch. X); the sources above use the 1914 numbering with the chapter title.
6. Works list (already recorded in works.report.md, not repeated here): kipling (Gutenberg 78240), rider-waite, dial-1922, pound-spirit-of-romance, tristan, tennyson, huxley.
