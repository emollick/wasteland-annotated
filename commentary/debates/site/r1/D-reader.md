## The batch as a whole

This batch is better than the two examples Ethan quoted: most records already say what the thing is and where it plays, and the Eliot recordings, the opera excerpts and the scans are nearly right as they stand. The weak group is the readers, where the body describes the performance in the reviewer's voice ("like a man telling you something he would rather not", "each participle dropped like a stone", "sound like the word it is") instead of telling me what I am about to hear, how much of the poem it is, and what state the sound is in. Two records bury the one thing a listener must know before clicking, that the player or the link is not the thing the title names (Irons and Atkins; the manuscript at the NYPL). Several extracts never say which lines they cover, and two readings never say whether they are the whole poem. Most of the fixes are a word or a sentence; only Irons and Atkins may need restructuring.
