## gotterdammerung
Correction: "the two-line form the librettos give it" is wrong; Wagner's printed poem (Schott librettos, 1874 and 1882) has no vocalise at all, and the vocal scores (Klindworth 1900, Schirmer 1904, checked on archive.org) spread a longer string of syllables among the three voices, so no printing gives Eliot's two lines as a unit. Replace "and in the two-line form the librettos give it is the one Eliot's Thames-daughters sing at 277–278 and 290–291" with "which Wagner's printed poem leaves out and the scores supply at length; Eliot's Thames-daughters sing two lines of it at 277–278 and 290–291".

## lyly-campaspe
Correction: "take the song's 'jug jug' and 'tereu'" makes this song Eliot's source, which Eliot nowhere says (his notes give none for these words) and which the same words in other Elizabethan poems (Barnfield's "Tereu, tereu! by and by", 1598, among them) leave open; Lyly is the editors' candidate. Replace "take the song's 'jug jug' and 'tereu'" with "use the same 'jug jug' and 'tereu'".
