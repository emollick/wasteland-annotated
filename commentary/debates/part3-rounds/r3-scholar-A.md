tiresias: Correct the last sentence. Pound's "make up yr. mind you Tiresias" stands against the typist's line, "Across her brain one half-formed thought may pass", with "may" struck through (facsimile p. 47); the clerk's "Perhaps a cheap house agent's clerk" drew a different comment, "Perhaps be damned" (Zeligs, Vassar thesis 2013, citing the facsimile and Lyndall Gordon; Vendler, Yale Review, ties the comment to Eliot's "perhaps" and "may"; this matches my memory of the facsimile). As phrased, the sentence attaches the comment to the wrong line and makes "whether he knew him or not" about the clerk. Replace with: "Pound, in the margin of the typescript, objected to the scene's hedging: beside "Perhaps a cheap house agent's clerk" he wrote "Perhaps be damned", and beside the typist's "one half-formed thought may pass" he told Eliot that Tiresias should "make up yr. mind" whether he knew or not."
