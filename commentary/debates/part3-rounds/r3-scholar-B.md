The rest stands: "as above" does refer to the note at 192 (Tempest I, ii), and lines 191–192 echo the same speech; Eliot's note is quoted exactly.

carthage: One correction, introduced by rewording. Replace with "it is generally assumed that he met the sermon there." Eliot's note at 309 is quoted accurately in its shortened form; Pusey's "cauldron" for sartago is right.
