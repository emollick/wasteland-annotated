### epigraph
Verdict: not yet
1. "Neither the <i>Criterion</i> printing of October 1922 nor the <i>Dial</i>'s of November carried Eliot's notes, but the <i>Dial</i> printed the epigraph, with a note from the editors that an edition "with annotations by Mr Eliot" was coming from Boni & Liveright. Leading with the <i>Dial</i> states only what was seen. Put instead: The <i>Dial</i> printing of November 1922 carried the epigraph but not Eliot's notes; its editors added a footnote that an edition "with annotations by Mr Eliot" was coming from Boni & Liveright. The <i>Criterion</i> printing of October 1922 had no notes either. The book followed in December 1922. ("Footnote" is the editor's own word in the decision line; "note" will do if the scholar prefers it.)
The rest of the note stands as it was in round two.
