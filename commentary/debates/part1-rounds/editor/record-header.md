# Part I, The Burial of the Dead: the debate record

Ethan's instruction (20 September 2026): the commentary was written in the voice of an LLM; set up Eliot scholar agents and reader agents to debate every section so that it is clear, good in its wording, and real.

How this was run. Every piece of commentary hung on the title, epigraph, dedication and lines 1 to 76 was put to two reviewers working independently: a scholar (briefed on the 1971 facsimile, Ricks and McCue, Southam, Rainey, Grover Smith, Kenner, Gardner, Gordon, Crawford, and the source texts, with the web to check them) and a reader (an attentive non-specialist, briefed to ask whether a note is clear, whether it helps with the line, whether a person would say it, and whether it shows off). The editor read both reports, drafted the rewrite, and sent it back to both; each said whether it was satisfied or what still failed; the editor revised; a third round where needed. On substance the scholar had the last word, on wording the reader. Points not settled are listed at the end.

The items fall in two groups. Glosses, ways in, pathway stops and the Part I drafts record are in Part I's own files and were rewritten in place. Library notes, voice labels, tarot card notes and pathway intros live in files shared across the site, which the thread "Debate: front matter and notes" owns; that thread ran its own debate on them at the same time and applied its rewrite to the shared files while this one's fourth batch was out (between 18:44 and 18:51 UTC). The second round on those items was therefore run against its text, and what this debate still had to add is in `shared-proposals-part1.md`. Two other things about the fourth batch: the scholar's single packet of twenty-two library notes stalled twice before it was reviewed in three parts (its three reports are printed as one below), and the reader's round-one verdicts on the library notes, voices and drafts records were given on the text as it stood before the other thread's rewrite.

Below, each item is given as it stood, then each side's report in each round, then the editor's decision and draft. The wording that went into the files is the last editor entry under each item; for the shared items it is the other thread's text with this debate's proposal, if any.

A fourth pass, after the record was first written. The front-matter thread's own scholar and reader had read all the pathway stops before that thread learned the stops belonged to the part threads; their reports (`debates/site/r1/F-scholar.md` and `F-reader.md`) were on the stops as they stood before this debate's rewrite, and most of what they flagged was already met. Nine stops in Part I still carried something they had found: a reading given as fact, a source named without a word of what it is, a lake placed in a city, a knowing aside. The editor drafted revisions (printed below as the editor's entry after round three), the batch 5 scholar and reader each gave one verdict per stop (round four), and the text applied at 19:38 UTC is the editor's entry after round four. Two thin captions on the Eyes and Nothing pathways, which that thread's reader also queried, are a question about the pathways' design that the front-matter thread has put to the build thread, and were left alone.
